Pounded Yam

Description

Pounded yam (known as iyan in Yoruba) is a traditional West African staple food classified as a "swallow".

It is smooth, elastic, dough-like, and similar in appearance to mashed potatoes, though heavier and stretchier. It is paired with any soup of your choice e,g, egusi soup.

Ingredients

  • White yam: Peeled, sliced, and boiled until soft.
  • Water: Used for boiling the yam and adjusting consistency during pounding.

Steps

Traditional Method

  1. Boil fresh white yam chunks
  2. Transfer them hot into a wooden mortar
  3. Rhythmically pound them with a heavy wooden pestle, adding small amounts of hot water until completely smooth.
